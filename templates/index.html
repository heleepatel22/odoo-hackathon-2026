Who We Are

VendorBridge was built to solve the manual chaos of traditional procurement. We realized that companies spend hundreds of hours managing spreadsheets, chasing email approvals, and losing track of invoices.

Our mission is to create a seamless, transparent bridge between your company and your suppliers. By digitizing every step of the supply chain, we help you reduce costs, enforce compliance, and make faster purchasing decisions.

Powerful Features

Everything you need to automate your procurement workflow from start to finish without any manual overhead.

Vendor Onboarding

Digitize supplier registrations with secure document uploads, compliance checking, and automated approval workflows.

Smart RFQs

Send Requests for Quotations to multiple vendors instantly. Collect bids securely and compare them side-by-side.

Bid Comparison

Auto-generate comparison matrices for incoming quotations to ensure you always pick the most cost-effective option.

Multi-Level Approvals

Set up robust hierarchy approvals based on budget thresholds. Never let an unauthorized purchase slip through again.

Purchase Orders

Generate precise, formatted POs with a single click and send them directly to vendors via email notifications.

Real-time Analytics

Track spending, measure vendor performance, and predict supply chain delays through our comprehensive insight dashboard.
